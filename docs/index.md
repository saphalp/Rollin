# Rollin Documentation

Rollin helps college students find things to do, meet other students, and share rides for little or no cost. Students can host an activity, join one nearby, and arrange a ride to get there.

These pages explain how the app works and how we built it. They are also a place for the team to keep setup instructions, decisions, and problems we ran into along the way.

## Start here

- [Getting Started](getting-started.md): install the Android demo/test build or run the project on your computer.
- [Development Workflow](development-workflow.md): work on a branch and send changes to `dev` for testing.
- [How the App Is Organized](architecture.md): find the screens, components, and services in the project.
- [Supabase and App Data](backend.md): understand where accounts, activities, messages, and rides are stored.

## What you can do in Rollin

- Create an account and [verify your email](features/authentication.md).
- Follow the [first-login tutorial](features/first-login-tutorial.md) to learn your way around.
- [Create or join activities](features/activity-creation-and-discovery.md) and browse campus events.
- [See your activities and rides on a calendar](features/calendar-agenda.md), check for [schedule conflicts](features/schedule-conflicts.md), and [export events](features/calendar-export.md).
- [Share photos tagged with an activity](features/activity-explore-posts.md).
- [Message people and activity groups](features/messaging-and-notifications.md).
- [Offer or request a ride](features/ride-sharing.md), follow [pickup directions](features/road-routing-setup.md), and [track an active ride](features/live-ride-tracking.md).
- [Edit your profile and connect with other students](features/user-profiles-and-connections.md).
- Leave [reviews](features/reviews.md) or [report a problem](features/reporting.md).

For account and privacy questions, see the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) and [account deletion page](account-deletion.md).
