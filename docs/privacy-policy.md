# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10/05/2026

## Who this policy covers

This policy describes how Rollin ("the app", "we") collects, uses, and shares information
when you use the Rollin mobile application. Rollin is operated by Rollin. Contact us at saphalpant2003@gmail.com with questions.

## Information we collect

### Account and profile information
When you create an account, we collect your email address, full name, university, major,
and an optional profile picture. We verify that your email belongs to an educational
institution as part of account setup.

### Location information
If you use ride-sharing features, we collect location information to plan pickup routes,
show a driver's live location to accepted passengers while a ride is in progress, and let
you search for nearby activities. Live location sharing is only active while you have the
app open during an in-progress ride.

### Driver and vehicle information
If you register as a driver, we collect your vehicle's make, model, year, color, and
license plate number. If you choose to verify your license, this information plus your
government ID is shared with Didit, our identity-verification provider, solely to confirm
your identity and license status. We do not store your ID photos ourselves; Didit's own
privacy policy governs their handling of that data.

### Activity, messaging, and social information
We collect the activities you create, join, or save; messages you send through in-app
conversations; and your follow/connection relationships with other users.

### Device and push notification information
We collect a device push-notification token so we can deliver notifications (e.g. new
messages, ride updates, activity reminders) through Firebase Cloud Messaging.

## How we use information

We use the information above to operate core app features: matching you with activities
and rides, displaying your profile to other users, delivering messages and notifications,
and verifying driver identity. We do not sell your personal information.

## How we share information

- **Supabase** (our backend provider) hosts our database, authentication, and file
  storage. 
- **Didit** processes identity-verification sessions for users who choose to register as
  a driver.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** (Google) delivers push notifications.
- Other Rollin users can see information you choose to make visible on your profile,
  activities you host or join, and messages you send them directly.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own advertising purposes.

## Data retention and deletion

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of
your account and associated data, contact us at saphalpant2003@gmail.com.

## Children's privacy

Rollin is intended for college-age users and is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by
updating the "Last updated" date above.
