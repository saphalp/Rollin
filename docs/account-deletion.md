# Delete Your Rollin Account

This page explains how to delete your Rollin account and the data associated with it.
Rollin is developed by Rollin.

## Delete your account in the app

1. Open Rollin and sign in.
2. Tap your profile picture at the top of the screen to open the menu.
3. Tap **Account Center**.
4. Under **Delete Account**, tap **Delete Account**.
5. Enter your password and confirm. Your account is deleted right away and you are signed out.

## Request deletion without the app

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot sign in, email
**saphalpant2003@gmail.com** from the email address on your Rollin account with the subject
**"Delete my Rollin account"**. We will confirm the request and delete your account within
30 days.

## What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes:

- Your account and sign-in details
- Your profile (name, university, major, profile picture, interests and social links)
- The activities you posted, your RSVPs and join requests
- Your follows, messages and notifications
- Your ride offers, ride requests and shared ride locations

Deletion cannot be undone.

## What may be kept

Information may be kept for a limited time where the law requires it, or where it is
needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud and abuse. Messages you sent may still be
visible to the other people in those conversations.

See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for more detail, or contact
saphalpant2003@gmail.com with questions.
