# Child Safety Standards

Rollin, developed by Rollin, has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation
(CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM). This page describes our standards and how we
enforce them.

## Who can use Rollin

Rollin is a community for college students. Every account must sign up with a university
email address, and the app is not intended for anyone under 18.

## What is prohibited

The following are strictly prohibited on Rollin, in profiles, activities, posts, photos,
messages and rides:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) of any kind, including drawn, generated or edited images
- Sexualizing minors in any way
- Grooming, or contacting a minor for sexual purposes
- Sextortion, or threatening to share intimate images of a minor
- Trafficking or soliciting minors
- Sharing links to, or offering to trade, any of the content above

## How to report

- **In the app:** open the menu on any profile, activity or post and tap **Report**.
  Choose the closest reason and describe the problem in the details. Reports are anonymous.
- **By email:** contact our child safety point of contact at **saphalpant2003@gmail.com**
  with the subject **"Child safety report"**. Include the username, activity or post, and
  what happened. Do not attach or forward abusive images.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. In the United
States, you can also report to the NCMEC CyberTipline at
[report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) or by calling 1-800-843-5678.

## What we do

When we find or receive a report of CSAE or CSAM, we:

- Review it promptly and remove the content
- Permanently ban the accounts involved
- Report the material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and
  cooperate with law enforcement, as required by law
- Preserve information as required by law to support investigations

## Compliance with laws

Rollin complies with all applicable child safety laws and regulations, including the
obligation to report apparent CSAM to NCMEC under 18 U.S.C. 2258A.
